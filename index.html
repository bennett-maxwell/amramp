Franchise Opportunity Profile

Amramp

A wheelchair-ramp franchise offering the sale and rental of modular ramps plus related accessibility products and services. Every fact on this page is sourced from the Amramp, LLC 2025 Franchise Disclosure Document (issued June 9, 2025), with Item and page citations.

Key Facts

Initial Franchise Fee

$49,250 per 1M residents

Pro rata per territory population: 1M = $49,250; 1.5M = $73,875; 2M = $98,500. Average territory about 3M persons. Fully earned, non-refundable. (FDD Item 5, p.3)

Estimated Initial Investment

$138,397 to $234,866

Not including optional real estate costs; assumes you operate from your personal residence. $68,170-$121,600 of the total goes to the franchisor or an affiliate. (FDD Item 7, p.8)

Royalty

3% to 12% of Gross Revenue

Variable per the Royalty Table: depends on the products and services sold and how long the business has operated in the territory. Paid monthly. (FDD Item 6, p.4)

Brand Fund Fee

1% to 3% of Gross Revenue

1% if prior-year revenue below $300,000; 2% if $300,000-$500,000; 3% if $500,000+. Drops to 2% on revenue above $1M in a year exceeding $1M. (FDD Item 6, p.5)

Local Advertising

3% to 6% of Gross Revenue

Spent monthly as directed: 6% (or $1,200 minimum) in year 1; 4% at $300k-$500k; 3% at $500k+. (FDD Item 6, p.5)

Opening Advertising

$5,500

Opening advertising and promotion spent in the first 90 days of operation. (FDD Item 6, p.4)

Territory

Protected (not exclusive)

Defined territory with typical population of about 2,000,000 (census-based). The franchisor will not license another AMRAMP business inside your Territory, but reserves other distribution channels for non-custom-ramp products. (FDD Item 12, p.22)

Minimum Revenue Standard

$20K/month, once per year

Must exceed $20,000 in monthly gross revenue at least once per year; $100,000 annual gross revenue per million in population. (FDD Item 12, p.22)

Term

10 years + 10-year renewal

Initial term 10 years; one additional consecutive 10-year renewal term; $10,000 renewal fee. (FDD Item 17, p.26)

Financing

May be offered

The franchisor may on occasion finance the Initial Franchise Fee. See Item 10. (FDD Items 5 and 10, pp.3,14)

HQ

Randolph, Massachusetts

358 North Street, Randolph, MA 02368. (800) 649-5215. Affiliate Gordon Industries, Inc. shares the address. (FDD cover; FDD Item 1, p.1)

System Size

54 US outlets

52 franchised + 2 company-owned (MA, WV) at 12/31/2024, up from 53 total at end of 2022. Zero terminations 2022-2024. (FDD Item 20, pp.33,36)

Litigation / Bankruptcy

None disclosed

No litigation or bankruptcy is required to be disclosed in Items 3 or 4. (FDD Items 3-4, p.3)

Seasonality

Not seasonal

The franchisor states selling is not seasonal. (FDD Item 1, p.2)

Risk Profile & Franchise Considerations

42 / 100 risk score

Assessment: Moderate. A litigation-free, non-seasonal accessibility-services system with a protected-territory model and low relative start-up cost, tempered by a fully variable royalty (up to 12%), a 3% Brand Fund on top, and four outlets ceasing operations 2022–2024. The affiliate also sells similar products in Massachusetts and Rhode Island.

Risk inputs (published weights):

  • Base: 50
  • Capital ceiling: +5 (investment max $234,866 ≥ $200k threshold; +10 only if ≥ $750k)
  • Fee burden: +5 (royalty max 12% + Brand Fund max 3% = 15% ≥ 12% threshold)
  • Seasonality: +0 (FDD Item 1: selling is not seasonal)
  • No litigation disclosed: −10
  • Zero terminations but 4 ceased operations 2022–2024: −5 (partial credit)
  • Unit growth: −3 (US outlets 53 → 54, 2022–2024: positive but below 10%)

Method: 50 + 5 + 5 + 0 − 10 − 5 − 3 = 42. Published so an independent verifier can recompute it. A franchise offer can only be made through a Franchise Disclosure Document.
